Mines & Megawatts — privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026. Applies to the Mines & Megawatts website (minesandmegawatts.com) and the Mines & Megawatts Android app (package com.jupiterrockets.minesandmegawatts), published by Jupiter Rockets (Joshua Ott).

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies set by us.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. We have no server that receives data from the app.
  • Search runs on your device. What you type is never sent anywhere.

Your location (optional)

The map has a "show my location" button. Only if you tap it, your device asks for permission, and your position is used on your device to center the map. It is not stored, logged or sent to us or anyone else. You can deny or revoke the permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Map tiles and data services

To draw the map, the app and website download map pictures and data directly from these public services. Like any web request, they receive your IP address and basic device information (such as browser or app version) and may keep standard server logs under their own policies:

  • Basemap tiles: OpenFreeMap (data © OpenStreetMap contributors)
  • Terrain (lidar shaded relief): USGS The National Map
  • Mined areas overlay: West Virginia Geological and Economic Survey (WVGES)
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) serves minesandmegawatts.com

The map data (data centers, mines, wells, minerals, power plants, cameras, places) is bundled inside the Android app or served as static files from minesandmegawatts.com. Links you tap to source records open those websites in your browser, under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is a general-audience reference map and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device (only map cache and settings).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
