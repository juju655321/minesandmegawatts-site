Mines & Megawatts — about, credits and privacy

A public beta map of West Virginia's data centers, coal, critical minerals, oil and gas wells, EV charging stations, license-plate cameras and power grid, plus a United States view of data centers by state, by Jupiter Rockets.

For information only. Not for engineering, legal, or investment decisions. Data center power and water figures are estimates and are labeled as such. ⚠ marks anything unverified.

Data credits

  • Oil and gas wells and mined areas: courtesy of the West Virginia Geological and Economic Survey (WVGES), including the Coal Bed Mapping Program. Well records come from WVDEP Office of Oil and Gas permits.
  • Coal mines: MSHA Mines data set (US public domain). Coal production: EIA (US public domain).
  • EV charging stations: U.S. DOE Alternative Fuels Data Center (US public domain), public and currently available electric stations in West Virginia, via the NREL Alternative Fuel Stations API.
  • Power plants: EIA-860M (US public domain). Electricity rates and sales: EIA.
  • Minerals: USGS MRDS (US public domain). Elevation: USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), public domain.
  • Data centers: DataCentersNearMe (ODbL 1.0) and DataCenterData (CC BY 4.0); WVDEP air permit R13-3713.
  • License-plate (ALPR) cameras: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0), the same data DeFlock shows. No plate data.
  • Transmission lines and basemap: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL 1.0); tiles by OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles; map engine MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3).
  • Counties: US Census Bureau cartographic boundaries (public domain). Search for towns and counties: US Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer Files (public domain).
  • US view: data center campuses from the DataCentersNearMe redistributable download (ODbL 1.0, 15 Sep 2026; 666 campuses withheld by the source), state industrial electricity rates from EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.A (Aug 2026, public domain), and US Census 2024 state boundaries (public domain).

Open data (ODbL)

The ODbL-derived layers are published as their own files so they can be reused under the same license: datacenters_odbl.geojson, alpr_odbl.geojson, transmission_odbl.geojson, us/datacenters_odbl.geojson (with ATTRIBUTION.txt) (ODbL 1.0, © OpenStreetMap contributors and DataCentersNearMe).

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no cookies and no analytics. Search runs on your device. The map loads tiles and data from the services credited above, and they may see your IP address as with any web request. Full policy: privacy.html.
